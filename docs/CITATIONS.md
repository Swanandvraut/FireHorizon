## 1. NASA FIRMS Satellite Datasets

1. **MODIS Active Fire Product (Collection 6.1):**
   - **Satellites:** Terra (EOS AM-1) & Aqua (EOS PM-1)
   - **Product:** MOD14 / MYD14 Active Fire / Thermal Anomalies
   - **Citation:** Giglio, L., Schroeder, W., & Justice, C. O. (2016). *The collection 6 MODIS active fire detection algorithm and fire products*. Remote Sensing of Environment, 178, 31-41.
   - **URL:** https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/

2. **VIIRS 375m Active Fire Product (Collection 2):**
   - **Satellites:** Suomi National Polar-orbiting Partnership (Suomi-NPP), NOAA-20 (JPSS-1), and NOAA-21 (JPSS-2)
   - **Product:** VNP14IMGTDL_NRT / VJ114IMGTDL_NRT / VJ214IMGTDL_NRT
   - **Citation:** Schroeder, W., Oliva, P., Giglio, L., & Csiszar, I. A. (2014). *The New VIIRS 375 m active fire detection data product: Algorithm description and initial assessment*. Remote Sensing of Environment, 143, 85-96.
   - **URL:** https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/content/descriptions/FIRMS_VIIRS_Firehotspots.html

## 3. Geospatial & Scientific Open Source Software

- **Database:** Supabase PostgreSQL with PostGIS and Row Level Security.
